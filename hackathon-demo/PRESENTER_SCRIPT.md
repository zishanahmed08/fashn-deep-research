# FashnAI — 2-Minute Presenter Script

Read-aloud script. Practice once out loud with a timer — most people naturally land close to 2:00 at a relaxed pace. Bracketed lines are what you click/show, not what you say.

---

**[Show: archify-diagram.html, full system view]**

"You find a product you want to buy. The brand's page says it's organic cotton, 4.8 stars, in stock. How much of that can you actually trust?

This is FashnAI — four AI agents that already compare prices, read reviews, and pull specs across fashion retailers. For this challenge, we gave it a fifth job: don't just report what the brand says — verify it."

**[Click into the live app → Due Diligence page]**

"I paste one product URL. Behind it, a planner breaks the question into three jobs — price, specs, reviews — and runs them in parallel against the live web, powered by Nebius Token Factory."

**[Submit. Let it run on screen. This is the one part you should NOT cut or fake.]**

"Every claim those three agents make gets handed to a fourth agent — a skeptical fact-checker. It searches Tavily, and it's explicitly forbidden from trusting the brand's own domain."

**[Point at the trace panel as it scrolls]**

"Watch the trace — this isn't a script, it's the agent deciding in real time. Right there: it just flagged a claim the brand can't actually back up."

**[Click "sources" on 2–3 claim cards, expand them]**

"Click any claim and you get the receipts: the agent's source and quote, and the independent source the fact-checker found that confirms — or contradicts — it. Nothing here is asserted without proof."

**[Scroll to the top-level verdict badge]**

"One verdict, fully sourced, built end-to-end on Nebius Token Factory and Tavily."

**[Back to archify-diagram.html, or a simple end slide]**

"That's FashnAI — agentic due diligence you can actually check. Thanks."

---

### Timing checkpoints (so you self-correct mid-presentation)
- 0:00–0:20 → should be mid-way through the hook, about to click into the app
- 0:45 → should be right as you hit submit
- 1:20 → should be mid-trace, pointing at the contested claim
- 1:45 → should be clicking into citations
- 2:00 → verdict + closing line

### If you're ahead of pace
Linger on one expanded citation and actually read the quote aloud — judges value that more than rushing to the next beat.

### If you're behind pace
Cut the "If you're ahead of pace" line above (obviously) and skip straight from the trace panel to the verdict badge — drop the citation-clicking beat to one claim instead of three.
